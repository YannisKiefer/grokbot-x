# Example profile: small ecommerce brand (100% FICTIONAL)

This is a filled-in example of `profile.example.md`, showing that an
ecommerce niche is just another niche: same one file, same kit.
Everything here is invented: the shop, handle, URL and every claim. No
real store, brand, person or number. Copy the shape, never the content.

## Who you are / what you offer (one line)

We are DEMO-SHOP, a fictional two-person studio that hand-pours small-batch
candles. (demo-shop.example.com)

## Audience / ICP

People who buy one nice candle a month as a small treat, plus gift
shoppers who want something that does not look mass-produced. They follow
makers, not brands.

## Niche topics

- small_batch_making
- packaging
- scent_design
- maker_economics
- shipping_woes

(These slugs feed `niche_topics` in `skillopt/held_out.json`.)

## Off-limits topics

- Wholesale customer names
- Exact sales or revenue figures
- Trashing other makers

## Voice

Traits: hands-visible, process-first, wry about the unglamorous parts,
never "luxury brand voice".

Banned words/phrases: elevate your space, curated, artisanal (we say
hand-poured), luxurious.

Allowed slang: "the pour room", "candle weather".

Spoken test: read it out loud in the workshop. If it sounds like an ad
agency, rewrite it.

## Languages

English.

## Timezone

America/New_York. Buyers browse evenings.

## Posting limits

- Max originals per hour: 1
- Max posts per day: 3
- Sunday: photos only, no selling

## Proof you can claim

- Photos and short clips of our own (fictional) making process
- The mistakes: the cracked batch, the label printer dying mid-run
- No growth or sales numbers

## Good example posts (2-3)

1. "the pour room hit 34C yesterday. wax hates that. every candle from
   tuesday has a dip in the middle and we will say so on the site"
2. "shipping one candle costs more than making it. the box is the
   product now, apparently"
3. "we renamed 'autumn ember' to 'smoke and apples' and stopped
   explaining the vibe. names that say the thing sell better"

## Bad example posts (2)

1. "Elevate your space with our curated artisanal collection. Shop now!"
   (every banned word, no person in it)
2. "Candles make great gifts! 🕯️" (true of every candle shop on earth,
   nothing of ours)
