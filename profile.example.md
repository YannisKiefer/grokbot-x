# Your profile (single source of truth)

Copy this file to `profile.md` (gitignored) and fill every blank.
Everything in this kit reads from `profile.md`: the skills, the playbook,
the draft voice, the scorer's `niche_topics`. Nothing here is pre-filled:
this kit works for any niche (SaaS, coaching, creator, agency, ecommerce,
anything). If a field does not apply, write "none" rather than deleting it.

Never commit the filled `profile.md`. Never put secrets, customer names or
private numbers in it.

## Who you are / what you offer (one line)

WHO_YOU_ARE — you, plus the thing you offer, in one plain line a stranger
understands. Example shape: "I am a WHO, I help AUDIENCE do THING."

## Audience / ICP

YOUR_AUDIENCE — who exactly you want to reach: their role or life stage,
the problem they already know they have, where they hang out on X. One
short paragraph or 3 bullets, max.

## Niche topics

- YOUR_TOPIC_1
- YOUR_TOPIC_2
- YOUR_TOPIC_3

The 3-8 topics you can post about with real authority. Short slugs, one
per line (e.g. "onboarding", "pricing", "home workouts"). These also feed
`niche_topics` in `skillopt/held_out.json` (the scorer halves the reward
of off-niche posts), so keep the slugs identical there: copy this list.

## Off-limits topics

YOUR_OFF_LIMITS — topics you never post about: client names, politics,
anything under NDA, your old employer's internals, whatever you decide.
The bot must respect this list without being reminded.

## Voice

3-5 traits: YOUR_TRAIT_1, YOUR_TRAIT_2, YOUR_TRAIT_3.

Banned words/phrases: YOUR_BANNED_WORDS (words you would never type).

Allowed slang: YOUR_ALLOWED_SLANG (the few casual words that ARE you, e.g.
"imo" — or "none").

Spoken test: if you would not say the line out loud to a peer, rewrite it.

## Languages

YOUR_LANGUAGES — the language(s) you post in. If your audience lives in
another language, say so; reply in the parent post's language when it
clears the bar.

## Timezone

YOUR_TIMEZONE — your base timezone, plus any window where your audience
scrolls (e.g. "evenings in YOUR_TIMEZONE").

## Posting limits

- Max originals per hour: YOUR_LIMIT (kit default: 1)
- Max posts per day: YOUR_LIMIT (start small, e.g. 5-15; the cadence doc
  explains why)
- Days off / quiet hours: YOUR_RULES

## Proof you can claim

- YOUR_PROOF_1 — only what you can back with a link or a screenshot.
- YOUR_PROOF_2 — no invented metrics, no borrowed numbers, no "we" if it
  is just you, until it is true.

Anything you cannot prove goes in Off-limits or stays unsaid.
