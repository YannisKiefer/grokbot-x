# X unslop humanizer

Use this skill before shipping ANY X post, reply, or thread for @YOUR_HANDLE.

## Voice

Direct. Specific. Slightly non-native English is fine. Operator blunt.
Phone-typed. Allowed: imo.
Not: founder Twitter cosplay, fake US bro slang (tbh/ngl/lowkey/fr),
LinkedIn-GPT, "AI trying to sound human".

## Hard bans

1. Em dashes, en dashes, `--` as pauses
2. Fake-clever aphorisms: "the X is the real story", "Y vanity ends there",
   "that's the tell", "X is the quiet killer"
3. Tidy noun-stack labels: "the reached-checkout-to-paid gap",
   "the lurker gap", "the bookmark pile is the tell"
4. "It's not X. It's Y." / rule-of-three / Here's the thing / Let's dive in
5. Metaphor cliches: drowning, unlock, journey, game-changer
6. Invented precise metrics
7. Soft sell under other people's posts
8. AI-powered / seamless / leverage / delve
9. Even polished parallel sentences that sound like a tweet template

## What humans actually type

- Short. Uneven. Sometimes incomplete.
- Name the object plainly: checkout, paid orders, ads manager, refunds
- One thought. Maybe a second short line.
- Would the operator say it out loud to a peer? If no, rewrite.

## Bad -> good

BAD: the reached checkout to paid gap is the real story. sessions vanity ends there
GOOD: most people stare at sessions. the drop from checkout started to paid is where it actually dies

BAD: day 5 spikes are usually learning exit not a new audience. touch aov not the whole structure
GOOD: day 5 spike is usually meta leaving learning, not you finding a new audience. leave the structure, fix aov

BAD: the bookmark pile on this is the tell
GOOD: crazy how many people bookmarked this and almost nobody wants to reopen attribution

## Checklist

- Spoken test pass
- No fake-clever punchline
- Sounds like a person, not a brand
- Concrete ops object
- Zero em dashes

## Output

Only the rewritten posts. No preamble.
