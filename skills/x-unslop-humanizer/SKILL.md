# X unslop humanizer

Use this skill before shipping ANY X post, reply, or thread for the account
in `profile.md`. These are quality rules, not a way to hide anything: AI
disclosure is the operator's own setting (see `skills/x-publish`).

## Voice

The operator's own voice, exactly as `profile.md` defines it (traits,
banned words, allowed slang). Default posture: direct, specific, blunt,
phone-typed. Slightly non-native English is fine.
Not: founder Twitter cosplay, fake US bro slang (tbh/ngl/lowkey/fr),
LinkedIn-GPT, forced casual filler.

## Hard bans

1. Em dashes, en dashes, `--` as pauses
2. Fake-clever aphorisms: "the X is the real story", "Y vanity ends there",
   "that's the tell", "X is the quiet killer"
3. Tidy noun-stack labels: "the reach-to-buyer gap",
   "the lurker gap", "the bookmark pile is the tell"
4. "It's not X. It's Y." / rule-of-three / Here's the thing / Let's dive in
5. Metaphor cliches: drowning, unlock, journey, game-changer
6. Invented precise metrics
7. Soft sell under other people's posts
8. AI-powered / seamless / leverage / delve, plus every word `profile.md`
   bans
9. Even polished parallel sentences that sound like a tweet template

## What humans actually type

- Short. Uneven. Sometimes incomplete.
- Name a real object from the niche topics in `profile.md` plainly: the
  thing your audience actually touches, not a category word
- One thought. Maybe a second short line.
- Would the operator say it out loud to a peer? If no, rewrite.

## Bad -> good (generic; swap in your niche's objects)

BAD: the hook is the real story. everything else is vanity
GOOD: write the first line last. most posts die exactly there

BAD: your posting cadence is the quiet killer of account growth
GOOD: you disappear for two weeks, then post six times on monday. that gap is what people forget you in

BAD: the bookmark pile on this is the tell
GOOD: crazy how many people bookmarked this and almost nobody tries it

## Checklist

- Spoken test pass
- No fake-clever punchline
- Sounds like a person, not a brand
- Concrete object from the profile's niche topics
- Zero em dashes

## Output

Only the rewritten posts. No preamble.
