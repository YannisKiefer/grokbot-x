# X article intent engine

Best opportunity is often not another random post. Someone else already
gathered your ICP and left out the operational next step. You add that
step, or you stay quiet.

Four decisions only: **Ignore** | **Save as research** | **Contextual reply**
| **Owned article**.

## Score before writing

Buyer fit. Pain strength. Missing next step. Comment permission.
Conversion path. Risk. Revenue potential (hypothesis, not a promise).

## Strong reply

Add the missing thought BEFORE any product mention. No spam plugs.
No reusable templates. Company voice, not a borrowed persona.

## Owned article

Operator POV. Category demo. Soft path to YOUR_PRODUCT. Not a brochure.

## Approval

Nothing in this engine ships without a decision packet: why this parent,
what you will say, what you will not say, what success looks like.

## Success

Buyer replies, profile visits, clicks, signups, upgrades, revenue.
Not likes.

## Kill

Attention without buyers. Account looks automated.

## Blocked

Mass replies. Auto-likes / auto-follows. Fake engagement. Browser spam.
Private proof posted as public. Forced plugs. Impersonation.
Guaranteed revenue claims.

## Pilot shape

20 candidates -> 5 shortlist -> 3 packets -> 1-3 published moves/day.
Quality over volume for THIS engine. Do not merge it with the gold-reply
volume motion.

The live loop (auto discovery, publish, readback, attribution) is on you
to prove. Do not claim it works until you can point at your own numbers.
