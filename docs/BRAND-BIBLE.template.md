# Brand bible worksheet (questions only)

The single source of truth for who you are is `profile.md` (copy
`profile.example.md` to `profile.md` and fill it). Most operators never
need more than that file.

This worksheet is the optional second layer: positioning depth for people
who want it. It is questions only. No answers are pre-filled, because
this kit works for any niche and your positioning is yours to write.
Answer in your own words, keep it honest, and delete any question that
does not apply to you. Never commit the filled copy.

## One line

1. If a stranger reads one sentence about you, what do they need to
   understand? Write it.
2. What do you actually offer, and to whom? No adjectives.
3. What is the one thing you want people to say about you when you are
   not in the room?

## Category and enemy

4. In one phrase, what category do you live in? (Whatever you pick, it
   must be instantly understandable to your audience.)
5. What is the default alternative people use instead of you? What does
   that alternative fail at?
6. What mistaken belief about your field do you argue against most often?

## Proof

7. What can you claim today with a link or a screenshot? List it.
8. What do people usually claim in your field that YOU cannot claim yet?
   (Those lines are banned until true.)
9. What has changed for a real person or business because of you, that
   they would confirm? (No numbers you cannot source.)

## Audience

10. Who exactly is this for? Role, situation, the problem they already
    know they have.
11. Who is it clearly NOT for?
12. What do they believe before they find you, and after?

## Voice

13. Three to five voice traits. Pull them from your `profile.md`.
14. Which words or phrases would never survive your edit?
15. Read your last ten posts out loud. Which ones sound like you? What do
    the others sound like?

## Content pillars

16. List 3-6 recurring themes you can carry for a year. (Your `profile.md`
    niche topics are the seed list.)
17. For each: what does your audience get that they cannot get from the
    default alternative?

## Objections

18. What is the first skeptical question people ask about your offer?
19. What is the honest answer, including the catch?

## Before you publish

20. Could this exact copy come from anyone in your category? If yes, what
    makes it yours — a named object, a real event, your own scar?
